Adding a one-sheet

Shape3:4 portrait
Size1086 × 1448 or larger
FormatWebP

Add the file, then add one entry to the SLATE list in index.html with the title, tagline and logline. The slug is the title in lower case with hyphens. Slate entries and their loglines are placeholder data.
